power tools{power drills, saw machine0}, transportation{small e-bikes, e-scooters,maybe evs?} and so on.
I guess in simple terms its nothing more than moving magnets. Think of a cup cut in half with strong permanent magnets on its outer rim{outer runner} and turn those around,
in way that is bldc motor. Now the question how do we spin it? Well, now instead of you having to spin those motors yourself, right there in the middle of the cup where there
